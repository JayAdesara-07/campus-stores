QUICK GUIDE

Information

A simple guide for anyone starting a shift at the equipment desk.

CS

Your job is to know four things

What item is out? Who has it now? When should it come back? What happened before?

01

Start at Overview

Check the number of items still out and the current people holding them. This is the quickest way to understand the desk at shift start.

02

Open a loan to see its trace

Each physical item has its own loan record. Open the trace to see checkout, custody transfers, notes, and return events in order.

03

Transfer means a new holder

When an item moves from one person to another, update the current holder and expected return time. The original borrower stays visible in the history.

04

Record the physical return

Only mark an item returned after it is physically back at the desk. This closes the loan and makes the asset available again.

Simple words used in this app

Use these definitions when explaining the desk to a new person.

Asset ID

The unique label on one physical item. Example: AD-12.

Loan

The record showing that one asset has been issued and has not yet been returned.

Original borrower

The person who first collected the item from the desk.

Current holder / custodian

The person who has the item right now.

Custody transfer

A recorded handoff from one person to another. It does not close the loan.

Trace / activity history

The ordered list of things that happened to the item. Old events are preserved.

!
Important prototype limitation

This version saves data in the current browser. It does not synchronize between two computers or staff accounts. A production version would need shared sign-in, a central database, real-time updates, and permission controls.
